# Sena legal pages — launch review

Prepared 23 September 2026 for the current private, static product preview. These are drafts, not a determination that the production SaaS complies with all applicable law.

## Complete before public launch

- Confirm Nile Africa Technologies Ltd.'s registered name, registered address, public legal/privacy contact, and security reporting channel.
- Confirm actual hosting/access-provider cookies, durations, technical-log retention, processing locations and international-transfer arrangements.
- Confirm any production collection, providers, lawful bases, retention schedule, access/deletion process, security measures, and breach-response process before collecting customer or guest data.
- Have Nigerian counsel review privacy, website terms, cookie and acceptable-use policies against actual operations.
- Before paid onboarding: prepare subscription/order terms including taxes, renewal, cancellation, refunds, support, liability, export and deletion; agree a controller/processor data processing agreement for guest data. Do not treat the website drafts as an executed SaaS contract.
- Connect a verified demo booking destination. No real enquiry, reservation or payment is currently submitted by this website.
