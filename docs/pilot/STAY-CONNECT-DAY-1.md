# Stay Connect — Day 1 checklist

## Start of day

- [ ] Sign in to Sena.
- [ ] Confirm the property is **Stay Connect Solutions LTD**.
- [ ] Review today's arrivals.
- [ ] Review room availability.
- [ ] Review housekeeping state.

## When a booking arrives

- [ ] Confirm the reservation appears in Sena.
- [ ] Confirm the room and dates.
- [ ] Confirm the guest information.
- [ ] Confirm the payment status.

## When a guest arrives

- [ ] Find the reservation.
- [ ] Assign or confirm the room.
- [ ] Record payment where appropriate.
- [ ] Check the guest in.

## When a guest leaves

- [ ] Check the guest out.
- [ ] Confirm the room requires cleaning.
- [ ] Complete the housekeeping turnover.
- [ ] Confirm the room becomes available.

## End of day

- [ ] Review reservations.
- [ ] Review payments.
- [ ] Review occupancy.
- [ ] Review owner totals.

## If something looks wrong

- [ ] Stop the affected workflow if continuing could create bad data.
- [ ] Record what happened.
- [ ] Capture a screenshot.
- [ ] Record the device and browser.
- [ ] Record the time.
- [ ] Escalate through the pilot incident process.
